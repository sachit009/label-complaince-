    The 6 Mandatory Declarations (Rule 6)
    Rule 6(1)(a)
    Manufacturer / Packer / Importer: Complete name and postal address with 6-digit PIN code + Country of Origin.
    Rule 6(1)(b)
    Common / Generic Name: Standard commodity name distinct from brand trade name.
    Rule 6(1)(c)
    Net Quantity in SI Units: Standard units (g, kg, ml, L, N). Illegal abbreviations like 'gms' or 'kgs' violate Section 36.
    Rule 6(1)(d)
    Month & Year of Manufacture: Format MM/YYYY or Month YYYY (MFD / PKD / Date of Import).
    Rule 6(1)(e)
    MRP & Unit Sale Price (USP): Maximum Retail Price with "(inclusive of all taxes)" + Unit Sale Price (2022 Amendment).
    Rule 6(1)(f)
    Consumer Care: Name, address, helpline telephone/toll-free number, and active email for complaints.

    Statutory Penalties (Legal Metrology Act, 2009)

    • First Offence: Fine up to ₹25,000 for non-standard or missing declarations.
    • Second Offence: Fine up to ₹50,000.
    • Subsequent Offences: Fine up to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year, or both.
    • Inspector Powers: Power to seize non-compliant commodities under Section 15.
