    Rule 6(1) Mandatory Declarations

    1. Rule 6(1)(a): Name & complete address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer + Country of Origin.
    2. Rule 6(1)(b): Generic or common name of the commodity.
    3. Rule 6(1)(c): Net quantity in standard SI metric units (g, kg, ml, L, N).
    4. Rule 6(1)(d): Month & Year of manufacture / packing (MFD/PKD).
    5. Rule 6(1)(e): Maximum Retail Price (MRP) with "(inclusive of all taxes)" + Unit Sale Price (USP).
    6. Rule 6(1)(f): Consumer care details (helpline, email, postal address).
